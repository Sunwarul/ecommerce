# Reports

**Reports → Sales Report** (permission *Report Index*; exports need *Report Export*).

## What is counted

Only **completed** orders, by sale date. Drafts, voided and trashed orders are excluded.

- **Net sales** – order totals including EMI, installment and late-fee charges.
- **Collected** – money received on these sales minus change.
- **Due**, **Discount**, **Avg. order**, **Items sold**, **Customers** (unique buyers), **Charges**.
- **Collections received in period** – all money received between the dates, including old dues and installments.

## Choosing the period

Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days, This week, Last week, Last 30 days, **This month** (default), Last month, This quarter, This year, Last year or **Custom range**. Grouping is automatic (daily / weekly / monthly) or chosen. Growth figures compare with the previous period of the same length.

## Filters

Branch (managers can choose *All branches*), cashier, sale type, payment status, payment method, category, brand, product and search. Click a customer to see only their sales.

## Tabs

- **Overview** – sales vs collected, sales by hour of day, payment methods, by sale type.
- **Products & categories** – top products, sales by category and brand.
- **Customers & staff** – top customers, sales by cashier.
- Detailed orders table.

## Exporting

PDF, CSV or Excel for: orders (detailed, up to 20,000 rows), period summary, products, categories, customers, payment methods and cashiers.

> **Daily routine:** at closing time open the report for *Today*, check **Collected** against the cash in the drawer plus bKash/bank statements, then close the POS session.
