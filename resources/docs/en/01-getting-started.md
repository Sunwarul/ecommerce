# Getting started

This manual explains how to set up your shop in the software and how to use it every day: products and stock, selling at the POS, collecting dues and installments, reports, SMS and your public website.

> **Tip:** Press the **User manual** button at the top of any screen to come back here. Use the search box on the left to find a topic quickly.

## Logging in

1. Open your shop's web address in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
2. Click **Log in** (top right of the website), enter your email and password.
3. Forgot your password? Click **Forgot your password?** and follow the email link. This needs the email (SMTP) settings to be filled in by your administrator.

Accounts are created by an administrator in **Users & access → Users**. Public sign-up is switched off.

## The screen

- **Left menu** – every module you are allowed to use. Items you have no permission for are hidden.
- **Top bar** – the **POS** and **Orders** shortcuts, the **User manual**, the **Branch** picker, **Language**, light/dark mode, **Appearance** (theme colours) and your profile menu (**Profile**, **General Settings**, **Log out**).
- **Inbox icon** – appears when you may read website contact messages; the red number is the count of unread messages.

## Changing the language

Click the globe (**Language**) and pick **বাংলা**, English, العربية, Español or हिन्दी. Your choice is saved to your profile and follows you to any computer. The shop's default language (for new users and website visitors) is set in **Settings → General Settings → Default language**.

When the interface is in Bangla you can also show **Bangla digits** (১২,৩৪৫.০০) and **lakh/crore grouping** – see the chapter *Settings & Bangla support*.

## Working in a branch

If your business has several branches, the **Branch** picker in the top bar shows the branch you are working in. Sales, payments and installment agreements are recorded against this branch.

> **Note:** Switching branch closes your open POS session automatically.

## The dashboard

The **Dashboard** shows today / this week / this month net sales (completed orders only, current branch), the best-selling product, a 7-day sales chart, quick actions, recent orders and a low-stock list (items with fewer than 10 in stock).
