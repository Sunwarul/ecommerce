# Settings & Bangla support

Open **Settings → General Settings** (permission *Setting Index* to view, *Setting Update* to save).

## General

- **Business name** – printed on invoices and PDFs, and used as `{shop_name}` in SMS.
- **Address**, **Phone** – printed on invoices.
- **Invoice footer text** – printed at the bottom of A4 and thermal invoices (e.g. return policy, bKash number).
- **Default language** – used for new users and for website visitors who have not picked a language. Choose **বাংলা** for a Bangla-first shop.
- **Timezone**, **Date format**, **Time format** – saved for reference.

## Bangla & appearance

This tab makes the software comfortable for Bangla readers.

### Fonts

- **Bangla font** – used for every Bangla letter in the app, on invoices and on the website. Choices: *Noto Sans Bengali* (default, very complete), *Hind Siliguri* (clean, popular on websites), *Baloo Da 2* (rounded, friendly), *Anek Bangla*, *Tiro Bangla* and *Noto Serif Bengali* (book style), *Mina*, or **SolaimanLipi / Kalpurush / Nikosh (installed on this PC)** – uses fonts already installed on the computer and downloads nothing.
- **English font** – used for English letters and numbers.
- **Bangla text size** – Bangla letters look smaller than English ones; increase to 105–115% if text is hard to read. Applies only while the interface is in Bangla.

The **Preview** box shows a sample invoice line with your choices. After saving a font change the page reloads to apply it everywhere.

### Numbers & currency

- **Show Bangla digits (০১২৩) in Bangla** – amounts, quantities and dates use Bangla digits while the interface is বাংলা. English users still see 0-9.
- **Digit grouping** – *1,234,567 (international)* or *12,34,567 (lakh / crore)*, the way amounts are written in Bangladesh and India.
- **Currency symbol** – e.g. `৳` or `Tk.`

> When the interface is in Bangla, the **Amount in words** line on the A4 invoice is written in Bangla, e.g. *এক লক্ষ পঁচিশ হাজার টাকা মাত্র*.

## Email (SMTP)

Needed for password-reset emails and website message notifications. Fill **Host**, **Port** (usually 587), **Username**, **Password**, **Encryption** (TLS), **From address** and **From name**. Your email provider (Gmail Workspace, Zoho, cPanel mail…) gives these values. Leave the password as `******` to keep the saved one.

## SMS

SMS gateways, templates and switches are in **SMS → SMS Settings** – see the *SMS* chapter.

## Logo, favicon and website

Logo and browser icon are uploaded in **Website → Website settings → Branding** – see the *Website* chapter.
