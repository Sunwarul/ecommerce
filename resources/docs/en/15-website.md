# Your website, blog, FAQ and contact messages

The software includes a public website for your customers: a landing page, blog, FAQ, a contact form and a Google Map. Visitors see it at your main address; staff log in from the **Log in** button.

## Website settings

**Website → Website settings** (permission *Website Manage*). First choose the language you are editing in **Editing texts for** – each language keeps its own text, and an empty field shows the built-in default text in that language.

- **Branding** – upload the **Logo** (also used in the app header and invoices) and **Browser icon (favicon)**; switch the **Public website** on or off. When off, the home page goes straight to the staff login.
- **Home page** – top banner (small label, title, subtitle, button text and link, banner image) and the **About** section (title, text, photo, footer description).
- **Contact & map** – phone, WhatsApp, email, the address to **send new messages to**, address and opening hours. A WhatsApp number shows a floating chat button.
- **Social & SEO** – Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, TikTok links and the **Meta description** shown by Google.
- **Sections** – switch the highlights, About, Blog, FAQ, Google Map and Contact form on or off.

## Adding the Google Map

1. Open Google Maps and find your shop.
2. Click **Share → Embed a map → Copy HTML**.
3. Paste it into **Website settings → Contact & map → Google Map** and **Save**. A preview appears.

You can also paste a normal google.com/maps link, an address (*Bashundhara City, Panthapath, Dhaka*) or coordinates (`23.7508, 90.3907`). Short `maps.app.goo.gl` links cannot be embedded – use the embed code instead.

## Blog

**Website → Blog posts** → **New post**: title, web address (made from the title; Bangla is written in English letters, e.g. *ঈদ অফার* → `eed-ofar`), short summary, content (rich editor with headings, lists, links, images and YouTube videos), status (*Draft* / *Published*), **Publish date** (a future date schedules the post), language (*All languages* or one language), cover image and SEO fields. Unsafe code pasted into a post is removed automatically.

Ideas: *Eid offers*, *How to choose the right AC size for your room*, *Refrigerator care tips*, *New arrivals*.

## FAQ

**Website → FAQs** → **New question**: question, answer, language (*All languages* or one) and **Show on website**. Drag rows to change the order. The first six appear on the home page; all appear on the FAQ page.

## Contact messages

When a visitor sends the contact form, the message appears in **Website → Contact messages** and the red number on the **inbox icon** in the top bar increases (permission *Contact Message Index*).

- Click a message to read it – it is marked read automatically.
- **Reply by email**, **Call** or **WhatsApp** straight from the message.
- Write an **Internal note** and tick **I have replied to this message** to keep track.
- Select several to mark read/unread or delete (deleting needs *Contact Message Delete*; deleted messages can be restored from Trash).
- If **Email (SMTP)** settings are filled in, each new message is also emailed to the address in Website settings.

**Spam protection:** each visitor may send 2 messages per minute and 6 per hour; one phone/email may send 5 per day; and at most 100 messages per hour reach the inbox in total. Messages from automated bots (hidden trap field, or submitted too fast) are discarded silently, and messages with many links are refused.
